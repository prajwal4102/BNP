What is VAV Connect?

One app for event work,
from application to attendance.

Concerts, college fests, weddings, conferences, marathons — every event needs hands, and finding trustworthy ones is hard. VAV Connect is the bridge: volunteers discover paid and unpaid event gigs nearby, organizers post shifts and pick from verified applicants, and check-ins on the day keep both sides honest.

🧭

Discover gigs nearby

Browse events around your city and apply in one tap — no calls, no middlemen, no group-chat chaos.

✅

Verified check-ins

On shift day, check in at the venue with a photo and location. Your hours are logged automatically — proof of work, built in.

📈

Reliability that pays off

Every completed shift grows a reliability score organizers trust, so the best workers get invited back first.

For volunteers

Turn free weekends into experience — and income.

  • Find event work near you that fits your schedule — perfect for students and anyone who wants flexible gigs.
  • Apply in one tap with a profile you build once.
  • Check in and out on shift days so every hour you work is on record.
  • Build a verified track record you can point to — your reliability score opens doors to bigger events.
For organizers

Staff your event with people who actually show up.

  • Post an event and its shifts in minutes; applications come to you.
  • Pick your crew from profiles with verified histories and reliability scores.
  • See who's on site in real time — check-ins carry a photo, timestamp, and GPS location.
  • Get a clean attendance report per event: who came, when, and for how long.
