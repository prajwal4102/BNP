How It Works
1

Colour

Kids pick an animal outline and colour it their way on an iPad station — or on paper sheets we scan in.

2

Release

One tap sends their creation into the jungle. The moment of release is the moment every parent hits record.

3

It Comes Alive

Their animal roams a lush animated jungle on the LED wall — moving, reacting, and sharing the screen with every other child's creation.

The Event Manager's Math

Why An
Experience?

Footfall & Dwell Time

A stage show holds a crowd for 30 minutes. An interactive attraction holds them all day — and dwell time is what sponsors and venues pay for.

Organic Reach

Every child's moment on the big screen is a video on a parent's phone. Your event markets itself on social media — for free.

Sponsor Real Estate

Logos inside the jungle, branded stations, themed animals — an experience gives sponsors placement that people actually look at.

Turnkey From BNP

LED wall, devices, software, power, and trained on-ground crew — delivered, operated, and packed up by one team you already trust.
